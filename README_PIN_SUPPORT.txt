Who2Call V1.1: Owners can encrypt new cards with either exactly six numeric digits or a passphrase of 12+ characters. Existing .scard cards remain compatible and still require their original passphrase. Recipients enter the exact PIN/passphrase used by the owner. Six-digit PINs provide substantially weaker resistance to offline guessing; use long passphrases for sensitive contacts. This change does not add Argon2 or change the existing PBKDF2/AES-GCM file format.
