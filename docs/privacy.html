Who2Call Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your contacts remain under your control. Who2Call helps you select and securely share important contact information with people you trust.

1. Introduction

Who2Call is an Android application that allows users to organize and share selected contact information with trusted individuals, particularly for emergency situations.

This Privacy Policy explains how Who2Call accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

2. Information We Access

With your permission, Who2Call accesses contacts stored on your Android device.

Contact information may include:

  • Contact names
  • Phone numbers
  • Other contact details supported by the application

This information is used to display contacts and allow you to select which contacts to include in a shared card.

3. How We Use Contact Information

Who2Call uses contact information to:

  • Display contacts available on your device.
  • Organize selected contacts into groups.
  • Create encrypted contact-sharing files.
  • Display contact information from files you unlock.

Who2Call does not automatically share your entire address book. You choose which contacts to include in each shared card.

4. Contact Sharing and Encryption

Who2Call allows you to create encrypted contact cards using the .scard file format.

You can share these files through other applications, such as email, messaging apps, or file-sharing services. Sharing is initiated by you.

Recipients must have the correct PIN or passphrase to unlock the encrypted contact card.

Anyone who obtains both the encrypted file and the correct PIN or passphrase may be able to access the contact information contained in that file. Short PINs generally provide less protection than strong passphrases.

When you share a file through another application, that application's privacy policy and data-handling practices also apply.

5. Data Storage

Who2Call is designed to operate without requiring an account or a Who2Call cloud service.

Contact information and generated or received contact cards are handled on your device or through locations and applications you choose for sharing or storage.

Who2Call does not operate a server that receives or stores your contact lists.

6. Data Collection and Third Parties

Who2Call does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect contact lists for advertising, profiling, or sale.

When you use Android's sharing features, the third-party application you select may process the shared file according to its own privacy policy.

7. Permissions

Who2Call requests access to device contacts to support contact selection and encrypted sharing.

You can revoke contact access through your Android device settings. Revoking permission may prevent contact-selection features from working.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can remove generated or received contact files from the locations where they are stored.

You can also uninstall Who2Call to remove application data stored within the app's private storage, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior.

Files already shared with other people or stored through third-party services are not automatically deleted when you uninstall Who2Call.

9. Children's Privacy

Who2Call is not specifically designed for children. Users should only share contact information when they have an appropriate reason and authorization to do so.

10. Security

Who2Call uses encryption to help protect contact information contained in shared cards.

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. We recommend using a strong passphrase, sharing files only with trusted recipients, and communicating passphrases through a separate secure channel.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact the Who2Call developer using the support email address provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
